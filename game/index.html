🔥 EMBER DUEL

A card duel of fire, tide, stone and gale.

Goal: reduce Cinder’s Life from 20 to 0 before she does the same to you.
Ember is your mana: it grows by 1 every turn (up to 10) and pays for cards.
Surge: play two cards of the same element in one turn — the second hits twice as hard.
Flame strikes +1 · Tide heals +1 · Stone guards +2 · Gale costs 1 less, hits 1 weaker. Backdraft: +2 vs a Gale card Cinder just played.
Click a card or press 1–7 to play it, Enter plays the selected card, Space ends your turn.

30 original cards · 4 elements · one readable rival: Cinder goes for the kill when she can see it, and turtles when she is hurt.

VICTORY!

Press R to play again.

HOW TO PLAY

  • Each duelist has 20 Life and Ember (mana). Ember grows by 1 every turn, up to 10.
  • On your turn, play any cards you can afford, then press End Turn. You draw 1 card each turn (hand limit 7).
  • Strike deals damage · Guard gives block that absorbs damage until your next turn · Heal restores Life.
  • Surge: the second card of the same element you play in one turn has its effect doubled. Chain cheap cards to set it up.
  • Element flavour: Flame strikes +1 damage, Tide heals +1, Stone guards +2 block, Gale cards cost 1 less Ember but hit 1 weaker. Backdraft strikes +2 damage against a Gale card Cinder just played.
  • Cinder is readable: she takes the kill when she sees it, guards and heals when hurt, and loves a Surge as much as you do.
  • Win by bringing Cinder’s Life to 0. If your deck runs out it is reshuffled from your discards.
  • Mouse: click a card to play it. Keyboard: 1–7 play a card, ← → select, Enter plays the selected card, Space ends your turn, R play again.
